Scan these QR codes with the RailTrack AI Inspector app

📱 How to Test:

  1. Open the RailTrack AI app and login as Inspector

  3. Point your camera at any QR code below
  4. The app will automatically detect and scan the code
  5. You'll see the asset information and can start an inspection
